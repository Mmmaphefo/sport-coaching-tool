Built for the sideline, not the spreadsheet

Kickstat covers the whole season — from naming your roster to running a full round-robin league.

Squad management

Keep a roster of athletes with position, squad number and contact details. Coaches manage it; assistants can view it.

Event scheduling

Set up matches and training with a date, location and duration. Events go live automatically at kickoff and close when time's up.

Live match logging

Record goals, assists, cards, saves and subs as they happen, for your squad or the opposition. Edit or undo any entry in real time.

Leagues and tournaments

Open a league for other coaches to join, and Kickstat builds the home-and-away fixture list and standings table for you.
